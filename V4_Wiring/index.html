Wiring the single-relay controller

One ESP32 (WROOM-32U, 38-pin USB-C DevKit) reads the box air and the AC’s coil, and one relay swaps the resistance the Frigidaire sees on its room-thermistor connector (CN3). Pin numbers match controller/cooler-v4.yaml.

Before you plug in the AC

  1. The JD-VCC jumper must be off the relay board (done) before VCC goes to 3.3 V.
  2. With the AC unplugged, meter the CN3 leads: about 35 kΩ with the relay open and about 13.6 kΩ with it closed.
  3. Check there is no continuity from either CN3 lead to the ESP32’s GND or 3V3. The AC board’s isolation from mains is unknown, so nothing on that side may touch the ESP32.
  4. Keep the relay and the resistor network away from the AC’s refrigerant lines. It runs on R32, which is flammable, and the manual warns against sparking switches nearby.

The rest of the bench and install checks are in RULES-v4.md.
